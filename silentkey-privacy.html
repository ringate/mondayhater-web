Privacy Policy — SilentKey

Last updated: 1/2/2026

SilentKey is an independent, offline-first password manager designed for people who want absolute control over their data. Privacy is a core principle of this app, not an afterthought.

This Privacy Policy explains what SilentKey does and does not do with your information.

1. Overview

  • No user accounts
  • No cloud services
  • No servers
  • No tracking
  • No analytics
  • No advertisements
  • No third-party SDKs

SilentKey operates entirely offline. All vault data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

2. Data Collection

SilentKey does not collect personal data.

SilentKey does not collect, store, transmit, or process:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Account credentials
  • Usage analytics
  • Device identifiers
  • IP addresses
  • Location data
  • Crash telemetry
  • Behavioral tracking data

3. Stored Data (On Your Device Only)

SilentKey stores all user-created data locally on your device, including:

  • Password entries
  • Secure notes
  • Tags and metadata
  • Encrypted vault files

All stored data is encrypted using strong cryptography and is accessible only after successful authentication.

4. Encryption & Security

  • Master password–protected vaults
  • Argon2id key derivation
  • AES-GCM authenticated encryption
  • OS-level secure storage for key protection
  • Encrypted, portable vault files

Your master password is never stored, logged, or transmitted.

5. Network Usage

SilentKey does not require an internet connection for normal operation and does not communicate with external servers for analytics, tracking, or storage.

6. Data Export & Backup

SilentKey allows you to manually export an encrypted vault file for backup or transfer purposes.

  • Exported files remain encrypted
  • Only your chosen password can unlock them
  • The developer does not receive exported files

7. Permissions & Autofill Services

Autofill Support

SilentKey provides Autofill services using platform frameworks:

  • Android Autofill Framework
  • iOS Password AutoFill framework

Autofill operates entirely on-device and only at your explicit request.

Other Permissions

  • Local file access (vault export and restore)
  • Biometric authentication (optional)

8. In-App Purchases

SilentKey offers optional in-app purchases to support development. These purchases do not unlock features or affect security.

9. Third-Party Services

SilentKey does not integrate third-party SDKs or services for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

10. Children’s Privacy

SilentKey does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Any update will never introduce tracking or data collection.
